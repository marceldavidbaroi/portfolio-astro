## Role Overview & Engineering Evolution

Joining **Proficient Information Systems** in November 2024 in a hybrid capacity, I began by mastering frontend architecture with **Vue 3, Quasar Framework, and TypeScript**. Over time, my responsibilities expanded into full-stack engineering—delivering scalable **PHP (Laravel/MVC) backends**, relational database schemas with **PostgreSQL/Supabase RLS**, and dual-database engines (MySQL + MongoDB) for high-throughput enterprise platforms.

### Key Milestones & Contributions

1. **Enterprise Frontend to Full-Stack Migration**: Engineered mission-critical workflows across [TradeflowBD ERP](/work/01-tradeflowbd) and [Customer Service Desk](/work/02-service-desk), transitioning from pure UI development into backend API design, asynchronous queue management, and automated IMAP email ingestion.
2. **Modular Packaging System**: Built and standardized the [Enterprise Modular Packaging Infrastructure](/work/05-modular-packaging), decoupling UI components into standalone, reusable npm packages with Vite library builds and SemVer automation.
3. **Team Hackathon Victory & Celebrations**: Collaborated with peers through high-intensity hackathon cycles, celebrating the delivery milestone with our team outing at Mawa in April 2025.
4. **25th Textech International Expo 2026**: Represented the company alongside CEO Reza Khan, demonstrating ERP capabilities live to garment industry leaders during a 4-day international expo.
